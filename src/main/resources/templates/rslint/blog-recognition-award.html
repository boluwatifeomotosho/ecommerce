RSL Receives Int'l Recognition Award

November 11, 2015 • Company

RSL International Limited was founded with the purpose to provide customers an end-to-end service for marketing and distribution of FMCG products.

RSL continues to grow in presence within the global FMCG market space, and its performance has been recognised with multiple accolades and awards — including recognition for being Invincible in General Trade.

These awards aren't marketing hardware for us — they're proof that the manufacturers we work with, the distributors we sell to, and the retailers we serve trust RSL to move their products with the care and consistency the brands deserve.

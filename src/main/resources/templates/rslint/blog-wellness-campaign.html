Nestlé campaign launched in Central and West Africa

November 11, 2015 • Industry

Nestlé in Central and West Africa has launched its 'Choose Wellness, Choose Nestlé' campaign to raise awareness about the company's nutrition, health and wellness impact in the region.

The new six-week campaign looks to provide Africans with insight about how Nestlé is playing an important part in their daily lives through its brands, products and services.

The company is also aiming to provide information and education to promote healthy diets and lifestyles.

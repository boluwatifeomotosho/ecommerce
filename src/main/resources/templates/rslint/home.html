Our Story

Built by Nigerians, for Nigerian trade.

In 2012, we started RSL International with a simple idea: give Nigerian retailers a distribution partner who actually understands them — one that treats a corner-shop owner buying a single carton with the same care as a national chain buying a truckload.

Fourteen years later, we're proud to be a trusted distribution partner for two of the world's largest FMCG manufacturers — and to have helped hundreds of local businesses grow alongside us.

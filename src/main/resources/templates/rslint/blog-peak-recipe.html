Peak Vegetable Juice Recipe Unveiled

November 11, 2015 • 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 small carrots
  • 1 medium cucumber
  • 6 medium fresh tomatoes
  • 1 tin or 1 sachet of Peak Sweetened Condensed Milk

Method of Preparation

  1. 1Wash all vegetables in salted water.
  2. 2Peel and slice carrots into round shapes and warm a little.
  3. 3Slice the cucumber into shapes.
  4. 4Wash the tomatoes and cut into shapes.
  5. 5Pour a glass of pre-boiled water into your blender.
  6. 6Pour all the vegetables into the blender, add your Peak sweetened condensed milk, and blend until fine and smooth.
  7. 7Use a clean sieve to strain the juice.
  8. 8Refrigerate immediately. Serve chilled in a wine glass with 2 slices of whole-wheat bread — or as a dessert after a meal.

Uses

  • Can be taken alone as a refreshing drink.
  • Can also be taken with bread as breakfast.

Health Benefits

  • Vegetables are high in vitamins and minerals — boosts immunity.
  • Coloured vegetables are high in Vitamin A — good for eye sight.
  • Peak milk is a complete milk — protein, vitamins, mineral salts to protect and repair the body.
Conclusion

Peak milk can be used in diversified ways to boost the quality of our diet.

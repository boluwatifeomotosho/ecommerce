Est. 2012

Rethinking wholesale, one carton at a time.

RSL International Limited was founded in 2012 with a mission to introduce innovation and change to the FMCG wholesale distribution industry — an industry that had, for decades, operated on outdated conventions.

The traditional markets — Oke Arin, Mushin, Tradefair — served their purpose. But they left real gaps: uncertainty around product quality, opaque pricing, and a lack of proper engagement with the manufacturers whose brands they moved.

We built RSL to close those gaps. Assured quality. Great deals. Proper supplier engagement. And a modern, convenient buying experience that respects the time of every retailer, distributor, and consumer we serve.

It is possible to have the best of both worlds — the trust of a global brand and the accessibility of your neighborhood shop. That's the business we built.
